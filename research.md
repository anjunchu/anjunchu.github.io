My research explores how controllable quantum systems can simulate many-body dynamics and improve precision measurement. I am especially interested in platforms where atomic, molecular, and optical physics gives clean access to strongly correlated behavior, collective spin dynamics, and entanglement-enhanced sensing.

## Themes

- **Quantum simulation:** engineering effective spin models and many-body Hamiltonians in experimentally accessible systems.
- **Quantum metrology:** using collective quantum states and cavity-mediated interactions to improve interferometric measurements.
- **Quantum information:** connecting microscopic control, effective Hamiltonians, and emergent dynamics.
